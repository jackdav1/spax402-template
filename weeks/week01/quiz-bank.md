# Week 1 quiz bank (core questions)

1. Many sports statistics have a lower bound of zero and no fixed upper bound. What shape does
   that tend to give their distributions, and why does that mean most players are "below
   average"?

2. Kevin Durant's points, rebounds, and assists per game all have different means and standard
   deviations. Why can't you compare their raw SDs to say which stat varies most, and what do
   you compute instead?

3. A team's standardized entropy on 3rd-and-short is 0.31. Say what that means in plain English
   to a coach, and describe what a team at 1.0 would be doing.

4. You plot a league's FG% distribution and it's clearly bimodal. What does that usually tell
   you about the data, and what would you do before computing a single league-wide average?

5. Your agent hands you a 32-team table it computed from 50,000 plays. Before you use it, what's
   the cheapest way to gain real confidence the pipeline is right — and why does your Thursday
   hand-build make that possible?
